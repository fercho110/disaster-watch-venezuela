# UNIT 4 ALIGNMENT

This redesign should feel like a visual school presentation, not an adult news article.

The language should stay close to the Unit 4 level and themes:

- disasters
- earthquake
- volcano
- hurricane
- tsunami
- lightning / storm
- rescue
- months / dates
- Past Continuous + Past Simple
- “What were you doing when it happened?”
- “Why did...?” / “Because...”
- short report/blog style
- visual comprehension and speaking/writing prompts

## Important scope rule
Do not add advanced earthquake terminology as required vocabulary.
Words such as “epicenter”, “hypocenter”, “seismic intensity”, “tectonic boundary” or technical engineering terms may appear only as optional facts if absolutely necessary, not as the Unit 4 vocabulary task.

## Text density rule
No section should begin with a large paragraph.
Prefer:
- 1 short intro line
- 3–4 cards
- one image/diagram
- one simple interaction

## Recommended reading level
Short sentences, familiar verbs, simple connectors and concrete vocabulary suitable for approximately 11-year-old English learners.
