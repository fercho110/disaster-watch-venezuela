Heading: Stay Safe!

BEFORE

## 7. UNIT 4 VOCABULARY
Heading: Disaster Words
Intro: Tap a card to learn the word.

Use only these core Unit 4 words in the main activity:
DISASTER — a very bad event that causes damage.
EARTHQUAKE — sudden shaking of the ground.
VOLCANO — a mountain that can erupt.
HURRICANE — a very strong storm with powerful winds.
TSUNAMI — a very large sea wave.
LIGHTNING — a bright flash of electricity in a storm.

Optional bonus card:
RESCUE — help someone in danger.

## 8. GRAMMAR TIME
Heading: What Were They Doing?

Grammar focus:
Past Continuous + Past Simple

Example 1:
People were sleeping when the earthquake started.

Example 2:
Families were having dinner when the ground shook.

Example 3:
Children were studying when the shaking began.

Mini interaction:
Choose the correct answer.

People ___ dinner when the earthquake started.
A. were having
B. had

Correct feedback:
Great job! “Were having” tells us what was happening at that moment.
